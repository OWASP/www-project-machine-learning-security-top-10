## ML03:2023 Model Inversion Attack - Cheatsheets

These OWASP Cheat Sheet Series pages cover practical controls that support the
model-access and monitoring guidance in ML03. They do not guarantee that a
model cannot reveal information about its training data.

- [Secure AI Model Ops](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Secure_AI_Model_Ops_Cheat_Sheet.html) — covers model inversion and extraction risks across the ML deployment lifecycle.
- [REST Security](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/REST_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html) — covers authentication, authorization, and protection against excessive use of prediction APIs.
- [Logging](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html) — covers monitoring suspicious use while excluding or masking sensitive data in logs.
